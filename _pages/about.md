Hi, this is Marilyn! I'm a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at the [University of Michigan](https://cse.engin.umich.edu/).

My research sits at the intersection of machine learning and computer systems. I'm interested in building the infrastructure behind large-scale AI, with a focus on distributed systems, performance engineering, and communication-efficient training and inference for modern AI workloads.

Before Michigan, I completed my undergrad in Computer Science at Purdue. This summer, I'm at Adobe as a Research Scientist/Engineer Intern, where I build performance profiling tools for distributed AI systems, making it easier to analyze multi-process workloads and improve digital forensics.

Outside of research, I enjoy traveling, trying new food, and painting.

Feel free to browse my [publications](/publications/), [talks](/talks/), or [CV](/files/CV.pdf) or feel free to reach out if you'd like to [connect](/contact/)!
