As an SRC Research Scholar, I'm researching communication-efficient systems for cross-region LLM inference through hardware–software co-design across geographically distributed data centers. I'm designing learned activation-compression techniques and adapter-based methods that reduce cross-region data movement while preserving model quality, and evaluating partitioning strategies for prefill, decode, and agentic inference workloads across latency, throughput, data movement, and model quality under heterogeneous compute and network constraints.

Presented as a poster at USENIX NSDI 2026 (with Maxwell Kumbong, Hermann Kumbong, Ertza Warraich, and Muhammad Shahbaz). A related line of this work, on reducing the network cost of cross-regional inference through learned activation compression, appears in our [NAIC @ SIGCOMM 2026 paper](/publications/#2026-07-01-naic-activation-compression) with Regan McDonald, Ertza Warraich, Annus Zulfiqar, and Muhammad Shahbaz.
