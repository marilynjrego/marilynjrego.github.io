As an SRC Research Scholar, I'm developing hardware-software co-design strategies for distributed LLM training and inference targeting throughput-oriented workloads. This includes model-level partitioning strategies for bandwidth-efficient, multi-region execution, and deployment frameworks that coordinate heterogeneous compute — cloud spot instances, distributed data centers — to reduce cost and maximize aggregate throughput.

Presented as a poster at USENIX NSDI 2026 (with Maxwell Kumbong, Hermann Kumbong, Ertza Warraich, and Muhammad Shahbaz). A related line of this work, on reducing the network cost of cross-regional inference through learned activation compression, appears in our NAIC 2026 paper with Regan McDonald, Ertza Warraich, Annus Zulfiqar, and Muhammad Shahbaz.
