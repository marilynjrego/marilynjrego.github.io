Through Purdue's Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowship (SURF), I evaluated open-source and commercial LLMs for their ability to generate fully accurate specifications for static verification, applying prompt engineering, chain-of-thought (CoT), and few-shot prompting to assess model effectiveness. I categorized VeriFast benchmarks, built tailored input datasets for training and testing, and designed qualitative analysis methodologies to evaluate LLM output quality.

This work was published at FORGE 2025, and an extended empirical study is under review at ISSRE 2026.
